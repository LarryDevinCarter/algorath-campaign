# Vale of First Light

An expedition was sent here by [[Characters/NPCs/Archmagister Vaelor Thorne|Archmagister Vaelor Thorne]]. Strange magical artifacts that create [[Bestiary/Monstrosities/Kruthik|Kruthik]] monstrosities were brought back from here. [[Characters/NPCs/Algorath/Groups/Cult of Rebirth/Ziothar|Ziothar]] threatened Vaelor not to return to the Vale of First Light.
