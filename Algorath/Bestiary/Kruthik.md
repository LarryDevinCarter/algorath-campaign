# Kruthik

Kruthik are a race of hive-dwelling monstrosities that live in underground tunnels, warrens, and areas near heat sources. They are chitin-armored reptilian-insect hybrids that hunt in packs and carve sprawling subterranean hives. They are burrowers and climbers with tremorsense and darkvision. They have a keen sense of smell, hunt in packs, and can fling spikes from their backs. They are dangerous in large numbers and prioritize feeding, breeding, and hive expansion.
