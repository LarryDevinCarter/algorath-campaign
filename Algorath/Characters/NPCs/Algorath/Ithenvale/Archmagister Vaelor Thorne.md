# Archmagister Vaelor Thorne

Leader of [[Factions/The Athenaeum|The Athenaeum]] and the city of [[Locations/Settlements/Piata|Piata]].

He sent an expedition to the [[Vale of First Light]] which returned strange, magical artifacts that create monstrosities ([[Bestiary/Monstrosities/Kruthik|Kruthik]]). He is hiding this fact. He was threatened by [[Characters/NPCs/Algorath/Groups/Cult of Rebirth/Ziothar|Ziothar]] not to return to the Vale of First Light.

He hired the heroes to eliminate the Kruthik, hoping to keep the city safe and keep his involvement in the outbreak a secret.
