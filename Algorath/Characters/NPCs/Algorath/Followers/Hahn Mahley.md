# Hahn Mahley

Student at [[Factions/The Athenaeum|The Athenaeum]]. He studied under [[Professor Holt|Professor Holt]] and sees him as a mentor. He was left in charge of cataloging and research pertaining to findings brought back from the first expedition to the [[Vale of First Light]]. He was therefore left in [[Algorath/Locations/Settlements/Motavos/Ithenvale/Piata|Piata]]. However, he was never allowed full access to all of the items.

He ventured into the Athenaeum basement to fight the [[Bestiary/Monstrosities/Kruthik|Kruthik]] with the party. He will likely ask the party to take him to Zema.
