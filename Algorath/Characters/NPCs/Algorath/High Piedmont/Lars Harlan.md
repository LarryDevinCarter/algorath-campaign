# Lars Harlan

Lars Harlan is a local blacksmith and the owner of [[Lars' Smithy]].

He has a daughter named [[Bess]].

Lars is well-liked by the townsfolk, who often wish for him to take leading roles in the town politics, but he refuses. "Power corrupts all those who possess it. Best I keep to my forge."

## Interactions with the Party
The players met him when he sold [[Characters/PCs/Maki|Maki]] her rapier. They asked him about the [[Bestiary/Monstrosities/Kruthik|Kruthik]] in the school.

The morning after they solved the Kruthik problem, Lars's daughter [[Bess]] was taken by goblins, and he asked the players to help.
